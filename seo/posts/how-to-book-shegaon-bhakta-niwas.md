# How to Book Shegaon Bhakta Niwas: Step-by-Step Pilgrim Accommodation Guide

Planning a spiritual visit to Shri Gajanan Maharaj Temple in Shegaon requires clear knowledge of accommodation options. The Shri Gajanan Maharaj Sansthan operates clean, tranquil, and donation-based Bhakta Niwas facilities for visiting devotees. This comprehensive guide explains the booking channels, rules, check-in requirements, and essential tips for a comfortable and respectful stay.

---

## 1. Overview of Bhakta Niwas Accommodation

The Sansthan's Bhakta Niwas facilities are built to provide simple, clean, and peaceful lodging for pilgrim families, senior citizens, and solo yatris. Designed with devotion and simplicity at their core, these complexes offer a calm atmosphere suitable for reflection and rest after temple darshan.

### Key Accommodation Features
- **Cleanliness & Maintenance**: Well-maintained rooms, corridors, and sanitation facilities.
- **Family-Friendly Environment**: Quiet atmosphere encouraging respectful conduct and spiritual focus.
- **Proximity to Temple**: Convenient access to temple precincts, Anand Sagar, and public transit.

---

## 2. Reservation Channels: How to Inquire & Reserve

Accommodations are allocated directly through official Sansthan channels to protect devotees from unauthorized third-party booking scams.

### Official Booking Channels
1. **WhatsApp Inquiry**: Send a message to the official helpline with your travel dates, guest count, and preferred property.
2. **Direct Telephone Helpline**: Call the Sansthan booking office to speak directly with support personnel.
3. **On-Site Counter Booking**: Rooms are allocated at the Bhakta Niwas booking counter based on daily availability for walk-in devotees.

*Important Notice: The Sansthan does not authorize private agents, travel brokers, or external payment gateways. Always verify booking details directly with the official helpline.*

---

## 3. Essential Preparation Before You Travel

To ensure a smooth check-in process, pilgrim families should prepare the following details prior to departure:

### Pre-Travel Checklist
- **Guest Identification**: Ensure every adult member of your group carries a valid original government-issued photo ID (such as Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License).
- **Travel Schedule Confirmation**: Finalize your arrival and departure dates, keeping in mind potential rush periods during Ekadashi, festival days, or holiday weekends.
- **Special Requirements**: If traveling with senior citizens, infants, or guests requiring ground-floor access, inform the helpline during your initial inquiry.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Room Inquiry & Check-in Process

### Step 1: Submit Your Request
Contact the helpline via WhatsApp or phone with your desired stay dates, number of adult guests and children, and choice of location (e.g., Shegaon main complex, Anand Vihar, or Visawa).

### Step 2: Verification of Availability
The booking desk verifies current occupancy for your travel dates and provides guidance on available room types and check-in procedures.

### Step 3: Arrival & Check-in Desk Formalities
Upon reaching the Bhakta Niwas counter, present your original ID proofs, complete the guest register entries, and receive your room key assignment.

### Step 4: Room Occupancy & Departure
Devotees are requested to maintain cleanliness, adhere to quiet hours, and follow check-out procedures so incoming pilgrims can be accommodated without delay.

---

## 5. Helpful Tips for Pilgrim Families

- **Arrive Early During Rush Periods**: During major religious festivals, room demand is high. Arriving early in the day improves counter availability.
- **Respectful Premises Etiquette**: Smoking, alcohol consumption, non-vegetarian food, and noisy activities are strictly prohibited across all Sansthan premises.
- **Packing Guidance**: Carry personal toiletries, modest traditional attire for temple darshan, and any necessary prescription medicines.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can rooms be booked via private travel apps?
No. Room inquiries and reservations are handled exclusively through official Sansthan channels and on-site counters.

### What documents are required during check-in?
Original government photo identification for all adult guests is required at the check-in desk.

### Are accommodations suitable for senior citizens?
Yes. Facilities are planned to support pilgrim families and senior citizens with accessible corridors and supportive staff.
